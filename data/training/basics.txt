Animals are living organisms.
Animals need energy to survive.
Animals can be divided into many different groups.
Mammals are animals that usually have hair or fur.
Dogs are mammals.
Cats are mammals.
Humans are mammals.
Birds have feathers.
Birds have beaks.
Fish live in water and use gills to breathe.
Reptiles usually have scales.
Snakes are reptiles.
Lizards are reptiles.
Amphibians can live on land and in water.
Frogs are amphibians.
Insects have six legs.
Ants are insects.
Bees are insects.
Spiders are not insects.
Spiders have eight legs.
Animals can eat plants, other animals, or both.
Herbivores mainly eat plants.
Carnivores mainly eat other animals.
Omnivores eat both plants and animals.
