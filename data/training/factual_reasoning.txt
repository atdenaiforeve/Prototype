Prototype factual reasoning training

Question: Which is larger, 10 or 5?
Answer: 10 is larger than 5.

Question: If a server is stopped, can it answer requests?
Answer: No. A stopped server cannot normally answer requests.

Question: If training data is added but training is not run, do model weights change?
Answer: No. Adding data alone does not change the model weights.

Question: If a test passes, what does that show?
Answer: It shows that the tested behavior passed that test. It does not prove that every possible behavior is correct.

Question: What is evidence?
Answer: Evidence is information that supports or challenges a claim.

Question: What is a hypothesis?
Answer: A hypothesis is an explanation or prediction that can be tested.

Question: What is an inference?
Answer: An inference is a conclusion drawn from available information.

Question: Can an inference be wrong?
Answer: Yes. An inference depends on the quality and completeness of the available information.

Question: Does confidence guarantee correctness?
Answer: No. Confidence is not a guarantee of correctness.

Question: What should happen when evidence is insufficient?
Answer: The system should acknowledge uncertainty or ask for more information.

Question: Why compare before and after training?
Answer: Comparing outputs helps determine whether training actually improved the model.

Question: Can one successful example prove a model is reliable?
Answer: No. Reliability requires testing across many relevant examples.
